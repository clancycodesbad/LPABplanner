## Standard protocol (for the two pending external reviewers, and for any future full-council re-run)

Each of the three auditors performs a **full, independent** audit against `quality/REQUIREMENTS.md`, `quality/CONTRACTS.md`, and `quality/QUALITY.md` — not a subset. No sharing of findings between auditors before triage.

**Guardrails (identical for every auditor):**
- Cite `file:line` for every finding.
- Do not assume code correctness because "the library/pattern is common" — this is a small, hand-written app, not a widely-vetted library; every line was written by one hobbyist maintainer plus AI assistance (per the README: "unashamedly vibe coded").
- For any finding involving a whitelist, enumeration, or "handles all cases" claim (e.g. the `group`/`type` field pairing, the `data/stats/index.js` term-key registry, the six-pattern `steps` dispatch in `ui-onboarding.js`), perform the two-list mechanical comparison (extract from code directly; do not trust REQUIREMENTS.md's or CONTRACTS.md's prose lists) before asserting completeness or a gap.
- A finding with only one auditor's support (minority finding) must be re-investigated at triage with a verification probe — not discarded by default.

**Mandatory scrutiny areas for this project specifically:**
1. Does `engine.js:81`'s exam-clash `currentTerm`-only scoping match the intent implied by README's "detects lecture and exam timetabling clashes" (no term-scoping caveat mentioned there)?
2. Does `getExamText` (`ui-board.js:19-43`) ever validate the string it displays through `parseExamDate`, or does it trust `subject.exam` verbatim?
3. Does anything enforce `subjects.js`'s `group`/`type` field agreement, and would `engine.js`'s graduation math (type-based) and the UI's styling (group-based) actually diverge if they disagreed?
4. Do `ui-board.js:handleDrop` and `ui-touch-dnd.js:onPointerUp` both risk losing a subject on a failed cross-semester move, and is there any rollback mechanism either does implement that the other was found to lack?
5. Does `ui-toolbar.js:26`'s `Object.keys(plan)...sort()` produce chronological or merely alphabetical semester ordering in the exported markdown?

## Effective council gating (this run: 1/3)

Per the skill's rule for an incomplete council: with only 1 of 3 auditors actually run, **no finding in the mandatory scrutiny areas above (all of which are whitelist/enumeration/dispatch-adjacent or completeness claims) may be triaged to "no confirmed defect" on prose alone.** Every finding from the in-house auditor pass was backed by a mechanical check (a directly-executed grep/read against the source, not a citation of another generated artifact) — see `quality/spec_audits/2026-09-26-auditor-1.md` for each finding's mechanical evidence. This satisfies the "mechanical verification acceptable even under an incomplete council" allowance. **Majority-rule reconciliation (which requires 2 of 3 agreeing/disagreeing) cannot be performed this run** — every finding below is therefore carried at "confirmed by 1/3, pending 2 more independent opinions" status, not "council-confirmed."

## Triage Process (to be re-run once external results return)

1. Merge all three auditors' findings by `file:line` + description similarity.
2. For findings confirmed by ≥2 of 3: high confidence, proceed to BUGS.md directly (pending Phase 5 challenge gate).
3. For minority (1 of 3) findings: run a verification probe (read the code, produce a passing/failing assertion) before confirming or rejecting.
4. For code-review (Phase 3) vs. spec-audit disagreements on the same finding: deploy a verification probe; do not default to either source being "more correct."
5. Update `quality/PROGRESS.md`'s BUG tracker with every spec-audit-confirmed code bug (this step was historically the most commonly skipped — do not skip it).
